# Delimiters in Prompt Engineering

## Objective

Explore how delimiters can help clearly separate instructions from the text that should be processed.

## Example Prompt

Translate the text between the triple backticks into English.

```text
Il prodotto è arrivato ieri, ma la confezione era danneggiata.
```

## Expected Output

The product arrived yesterday, but the packaging was damaged.

## What I Learned

Delimiters help structure prompts and clearly distinguish instructions from the content provided to the model, ensuring that the model processes only the intended text.
