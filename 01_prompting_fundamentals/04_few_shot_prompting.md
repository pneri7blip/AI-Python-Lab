# Few-Shot Prompting

## Objective

Explore how providing examples can guide an AI model to follow a specific pattern when generating responses.

## Example Prompt

Classify the sentiment of each customer review as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

Review: "The product arrived quickly and works perfectly."
Sentiment: Positive

Review: "The item stopped working after two days."
Sentiment: Negative

Review: "The product is acceptable for the price."
Sentiment:

## Expected Output

Neutral

## What I Learned

Providing examples directly in the prompt helps the model understand the expected pattern and produce more consistent results. This technique is known as few-shot prompting.
